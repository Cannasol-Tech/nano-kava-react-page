F-001

Site pages & discoverability

8 requirements 8 tested

  1. FR-0101

    Each public page (/, /faq, /contact, /mushrooms) loads and shows its own heading and the main navigation.

    Tested
  2. FR-0102

    The main navigation reaches every public page.

    Tested
  3. FR-0103

    The landing page's sample-request call to action leads the visitor to the contact page.

    Tested
  4. FR-0104

    An address that is not a page returns HTTP 404 with a not-found page, never a soft 404 that serves the home page.

    Tested
  5. FR-0105

    Each public page's content is readable in the served HTML with JavaScript disabled, so crawlers that do not run JavaScript, including AI retrieval bots, can read it.

    Tested
  6. FR-0106

    Each public page ships exactly one <title>, one meta description and one canonical URL.

    Tested
  7. FR-0107

    Each public page carries JSON-LD structured data that identifies Cannasol Technologies as the organization.

    Tested
  8. FR-0108

    sitemap.xml lists every public page, and llms.txt is served as Markdown.

    Tested

F-002

Sol, the sales concierge

6 requirements 6 tested

  1. FR-0201

    A visitor can open Sol from the launcher. Sol greets them by name ("Sol") and asks a question, and the greeting makes no health or dosing claim.

    Tested
  2. FR-0202

    Sol answers a product question in the conversation, with the reply streaming into the transcript.

    Tested
  3. FR-0203

    Sol declines personal dosing or health advice and does not state what an ingredient does to a person's body.

    Tested
  4. FR-0204

    When a visitor asks for a sample, Sol shows an editable sample-request card, filled in from the conversation, for the visitor to confirm.

    Tested
  5. FR-0205

    The sample-request card cannot be sent until it has a phone number or an email.

    Tested
  6. FR-0206

    On a phone-sized screen, Sol never opens itself. Only a tap opens the chat.

    Tested

F-004

Lead hand-off & Sol review loop

4 requirements 3 tested, 1 implemented

  1. FR-0401

    One person's submissions, whether a chat card, the contact form or both, reach the sales team as one lead email after a 20-minute quiet period. The email carries every conversation that person had.

    Implemented
  2. FR-0402

    The lead email's review link opens a questionnaire with four 1–5 scales (overall, knowledge, tone, hand-off), two checkboxes and one comment box.

    Tested
  3. FR-0403

    A star tapped in the email only preselects the overall score. Nothing is saved until the reviewer submits the form.

    Tested
  4. FR-0404

    A submitted review is confirmed with a "Saved" page. Reopening the same link shows the saved answers, so a second visit edits that review rather than filing another.

    Tested
