### 01 · Why nano changes the math

**More effect per milligram.** Packaging kavalactones in ~20 nm particles multiplies surface
area, so the body absorbs far more of what you put in. You can formulate at roughly a quarter of
a conventional dose.

**Crystal clear, manageable flavor.** Dissolves completely into the beverage with no cloud, no
ring, and minimal kava-characteristic taste. No need to employ bitter blockers or flavor
modifiers.

**Drop-in simple.** No high-shear equipment, no pre-mix, no heat step. Batch your beverage, meter
in the emulsion, stir to disperse. That is the whole process.

### 02 · Where it comes from

We import kava extract directly from the South Pacific — grown in Vanuatu and CO₂ extracted
through New Zealand — then nanoemulsify it in-house.

Cannasol Technologies was the first company to nano-emulsify kava, over four to five years ago,
and has spent that time scaling the process and bringing cost down.

### 04 · Labeling guidelines

Brands dosing in the 50–60 mg range often lead with the weight of the whole nano kava complex
rather than the kavalactone figure alone — a 60 mg dose becomes "360 mg Nano Kava Complex."

The actual kavalactone content is then disclosed in the Supplement Facts panel, where a consumer
or a buyer's regulatory team can find it.

The panel shown follows 21 CFR 101.36: no zero-amount rows, plant part named, kavalactone content
disclosed, CRN caution wording (abridged). Every ingredient must appear in Other Ingredients —
have your own label reviewed before print.
