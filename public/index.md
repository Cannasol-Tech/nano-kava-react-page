## What is nano kava?
<a id="what-is-nano-kava"></a>

Nano kava is a nanoemulsified form of kava extract in which the particles are reduced
to approximately 18 nanometers. Cannasol achieves this with its proprietary
NanoOptimizer™ surfactant system combined with ultrasonic processing. The ultra-small
droplet size dramatically increases the bioavailability of the kavalactones — up to
10x versus traditional kava — and cuts onset time to roughly 5 minutes.

The terms **nano kava**, **nano-kava**, **nano emulsified kava**, **nanoemulsified
kava** and **kava nanoemulsion** all describe this same product.

## Nanoemulsification
<a id="nanoemulsification"></a>

Nanoemulsification is the process of breaking oil-based kavalactones into nanometer-scale
droplets suspended in water, so small the mixture becomes almost transparent. Cannasol
runs this process on QSonica ultrasonic liquid-processing equipment — QSonica is the
number-one name in ultrasonic liquid processing. The enormous increase in surface area
is what makes the kavalactones readily absorbable by the body.

## Kavalactone
<a id="kavalactone"></a>

Kavalactones are the active compound class in the kava plant (*Piper methysticum*),
responsible for its calming, stress-reducing effects. In traditional preparations they
are hydrophobic and poorly absorbed. Nanoemulsification is what makes them behave like
a water-soluble ingredient.

## NanoOptimizer™
<a id="nanooptimizer"></a>

NanoOptimizer™ is Cannasol's proprietary blend of food-grade surfactants that
encapsulates the kava droplets, keeps them water-soluble, and prevents them from
re-aggregating. It is the reason the emulsion stays suspended indefinitely with no
separation, no settling and no ring around the bottle.
