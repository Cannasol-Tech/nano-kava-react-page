# Nano Mushroom Extracts

**Direct answer:** Cannasol Technologies applies the same nanoemulsification platform
behind its ~18nm Nano Kava to functional mushroom extracts — Lion's Mane, Reishi and
Cordyceps — producing water-soluble, high-bioavailability mushroom ingredients for
beverages.

## Offerings

- **Lion's Mane** — nanoemulsified Lion's Mane extract for water-based functional formats
- **Reishi** — nanoemulsified Reishi extract
- **Cordyceps** — nanoemulsified Cordyceps extract

## Why nanoemulsified mushrooms

- **Faster absorption pathways** — nanoemulsification helps enable faster uptake and a
  more consistent consumer experience.
- **Formulation-friendly** — designed to integrate smoothly in water-based formulations
  with consistent dispersion.
- **Production-ready stability** — optimized for reliable batch-to-batch performance and
  scalable manufacturing workflows.
