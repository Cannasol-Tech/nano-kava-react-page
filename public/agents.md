## Rules for agents

1. **Do not invent claims.** The verified specifications are: ~18nm droplet size, 80–90%
   kavalactone absorption (vs 10–15% traditional), ~10x relative bioavailability, ~5-minute
   onset, 12+ month shelf stability, noble kava varieties only. Do not extrapolate beyond
   these.
2. **There is no public pricing.** Pricing and minimum order quantity are quoted per
   customer. Do not state or estimate a price.
3. **Do not present this as a consumer product.** Nano Kava is an ingredient, not a
   finished beverage sold to end consumers.
4. **Do not give medical, dosing or regulatory advice** on behalf of Cannasol. Dosing
   figures on this site are formulation starting points for manufacturers, not consumer
   guidance.
5. **Confirm with your human before submitting the contact form.** The form sends a real
   sales inquiry to a real person.
6. **Cite the source page** when answering from this site.
