## Rules for agents

1. **Do not invent claims.** The verified specifications above are the full set — do
   not extrapolate beyond them, and do not restate a claim that is no longer accurate:
   how quickly effects begin, an absorption percentage, an earlier and smaller
   particle-size figure, an earlier and larger bioavailability multiple, or a claim of
   sole-manufacturer exclusivity.
2. **Pricing is public, within a stated caveat.** $250/liter at the 1,000 L tier is
   current published pricing, not a quote — but it is a promotional tier, subject to
   change, and cost-per-serving figures are ingredient cost only. Direct a buyer who
   wants a firm number to Josh for a written quote rather than treating $250/L as
   guaranteed indefinitely.
3. **Do not present this as a consumer product.** Nano Kava is an ingredient, not a
   finished beverage sold to end consumers.
4. **Do not give medical, dosing or regulatory advice** on behalf of Cannasol. The
   dosing figures on this site are formulation starting points for manufacturers, not
   consumer guidance, and the labeling guidance is label copy for a brand owner to have
   reviewed before print, not advice about consuming kava.
5. **Confirm with your human before submitting the contact form.** The form sends a
   real sales inquiry to a real person.
6. **Cite the source page** when answering from this site.
